Amazon SQS at-least-once delivery Amazon SQS stores copies of your messages on multiple servers for redundancy and high availability. On rare occasions, one of the servers that stores a copy of a message might be unavailable when you receive or delete a message. If this occurs, the copy of the message isn't deleted on the server that is unavailable, and you might get that message copy again when you receive messages.
